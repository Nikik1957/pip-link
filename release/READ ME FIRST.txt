PIP LINK  -  setup for a Pip-Boy 3000 + Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3
=============================================================

Double-click "PIP LINK Setup.exe". There is nothing to install.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That happens because
the program isn't from the Microsoft Store. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

The program has three steps. Do them in order:

 1  PIP LINK UNIT    Put the new firmware on the XIAO board.
                     Plug the board into the PC, hold its B button, tap its R button,
                     then click INSTALL FIRMWARE. Afterwards, tap R once.

 2  PIP-BOY          Plug the Pip-Boy into the PC and switch it on. Pick your time zone,
                     then click INSTALL ON PIP-BOY. Restart the Pip-Boy once afterwards.

 3  MAPS             FIND your town (or county, or state) - or RIGHT-drag a box on the
                     map - pick how close you want to zoom, and click ADD TO MAP.
                     Maps grow: adding more areas, or more detail in a small area,
                     only adds to the map - nothing already built is lost.
                     Big areas: get the state's OFFLINE DATA first (Nevada: 120 MB).
                     A stopped build continues with AREAS > RESUME.
                     Then SEND TO PIP-BOY (USB - fine for a town, about 10 minutes)
                     or COPY TO SD CARD (big maps: put the Pip-Boy's card in a card
                     reader). Both continue where they stopped if interrupted.
                     PREVIEW shows how an area will look with the options picked,
                     before anything is built or saved.
                     PINS: add Google Earth pins (ADD KMZ/KML), then check them on the
                     map - click one to fix its icon or name, DELETE it, DUPLICATES
                     finds repeats, NEW PIN adds one where you click. They go into the
                     Pip-Boy's GO TO list (with your fixes) when the map is sent.

Then connect the PIP LINK unit to the Pip-Boy with the powered USB-C cable.
The Pip-Boy switches to PIP LINK by itself. Pick your radio from the list:
  MeshCore radios     type the radio's Bluetooth PIN
  Meshtastic radios   PIP LINK connects first; if the radio asks, type the PIN it
                      shows on its screen (or 123456 if it has no screen)
One radio at a time. You can switch radios later in SETTINGS > LoRa radio.

On the Pip-Boy:
  Side switch              picks the tab: MESH / MAP / RADIATION / TERM / SETTINGS (top to bottom)
  Knob 2 (3 clicks)        also changes tab
  STATS                    open the most recent chat (press again: back to the MESH list)
  ITEMS / DATA             jump to MAP / RADIATION (the button lights up on its tab)
  A blinking button        something is waiting behind it - press it:
                             STATS  unread messages: opens the newest one
                             ITEMS  someone shared a location: the map, centred on it
                             DATA   the radiation alarm went off: the RADIATION page
  Hold STATS               leave PIP LINK, back to the Pip-Boy's own menus
  Scroll wheel             move up and down; press the wheel to select
  MESH tab                 your channels, your favorite contacts, then CONTACTS: every contact
                           (favorites first, then most recent). Press on one for MESSAGE /
                           FAVORITE / REMOVE FROM RADIO (click twice to remove). A MeshCore radio
                           that adds contacts automatically adds it back when it hears it again.
                           ADVERTISE: your radio announces itself to the mesh right away.
  RADIATION tab            dose rate in rads, like the game (urad/h, mrad/h, rad/h);
                           normal background is about 10 urad/h
  On the MAP tab           press the wheel to open the menu: ZOOM / PAN / CONTACTS / GO TO / CENTER ON ME
                           (turn knob 2 to choose, press the wheel to pick)
                           ZOOM: turn the scroll wheel to zoom in or out
                           PAN: the scroll wheel moves north/south, knob 2 moves east/west,
                           in small steps (the map slides over at once, the new edge fills
                           in a moment later); press the wheel when you're done
                           A small crosshair marks the middle of the map while you look around.
                           GO TO opens your list of saved locations (nearest first):
                             NEW            save the spot under the crosshair: name it, pick an icon
                             MANUAL ENTRY   type a position like 35.8325,-115.4319 to look there
                             pick a location:  GO TO   centre the map on it
                                               NAV     dotted line from you to it, with the
                                                       distance and compass direction (azimuth);
                                                       it gets a * and moves to the top of the list.
                                                       Pick it again for STOP NAV.
                                               SHARE   send it to a channel or contact. Other
                                                       PIP LINKs beep and add it to their list.
                                               DELETE  remove it
                           Knob 2 goes back a step.
  TERM tab                 connect to a computer on your Wi-Fi (e.g. a Raspberry Pi)
                             WI-FI NETWORK    pick your network, type its password
                             NEW CONNECTION   pi@raspberrypi.local  = SSH (type user@computer)
                                              raspberrypi.local     = Telnet
                                              bbs.example.com:2323  = another port
                           Pick a saved computer for CONNECT / DELETE / MODE. MODE BBS (the
                           Telnet default) shows BBS art at 80x24; MODE STANDARD is
                           for Linux computers like a Raspberry Pi.
                           SSH: the first time, check and ACCEPT the computer's key; then type
                           your password (asked each time, never saved). If PIP LINK ever says
                           the key CHANGED, don't log in unless you know the computer was reinstalled.
                           In a session the scroll wheel scrolls back through earlier text
                           (press it to jump back to the live screen). Pressing it opens
                           TYPE / CTRL-C / ESC / TAB / SIZE / WHEEL / DISCONNECT.
                             SIZE    text size: LARGE 58x12, MEDIUM 66x16, SMALL 78x24
                             WHEEL   make the wheel send Up/Down keys instead (menus, nano)
                           A Bluetooth keyboard types straight into the computer.
                           Wi-Fi is only on while you use this tab.
  SETTINGS                 Theme: PIP-BOY uses the colour picked in the Pip-Boy's own settings
                           (presets or a custom colour); GREEN or AMBER override it while
                           PIP LINK is open
                           Map locations: SHOWN / HIDDEN (your saved locations on the map)
                           Tab wheel / Scroll wheel: how many clicks make one step (also
                           used by the on-screen keyboard)
                           Text margin: if the first letters of SSH / Telnet text are cut
                           off at the left edge, click to move the text right (0-12 px)
                           Keyboard: pick a Bluetooth keyboard. It has to be in pairing mode
                           to show up - a Logitech keyboard: press an Easy-Switch key (1-3),
                           then hold it about 3 s until it blinks fast. A keyboard that is
                           still paired to another PIP LINK, phone or PC stays hidden until
                           then. Choosing (none) unpairs it from this PIP LINK.
  On-screen keyboard       scroll wheel: row, knob 2: column, press: type the key. The
                           highlight stops at the edges. CANC asks before throwing away
                           your text (turn to YES, press).

With a Bluetooth LE keyboard (pair it in SETTINGS > Keyboard):
  Left / Right / Tab       change tab          1 - 5      jump to a tab
  Up / Down                move                Enter      select       Esc   back
  In a chat                just start typing to reply, Enter sends
  On the MAP tab           Up/Down or + / -  zoom      P  pan with the arrow keys (Enter = done)
                           C  contacts   G  GO TO list   H  back to your position

Updates: when you get a file called PIP-LINK-update-v....zip, open the UPDATES page,
click LOAD UPDATE FILE, then do steps 1 and 2 again. Your settings and maps are kept.
